Brand System — Identity & Motion. A living document, v1.0 — 2026

Make the interface disappear. Let the ideas become the interface.

This is not a rulebook. It is a description of how YOWO behaves — on a page, on a screen, in motion. Everything follows one belief: the brand never explains itself. The work speaks, the canvas stays white, and the ecosystem is quietly alive.

02

Wordmark

The wordmark is typographic only. Two registered forms exist — the tracked mono mark for navigation and small contexts, and the display mark for endings and large statements. There is no symbol; the flora cursor is the only living emblem, and it never freezes into a logo.

Primary mark YOWO Mono 700 / tracking 0.34em
Display mark YOWO Grotesk 500 / tracking 0.06em
Clearspace = height of one “O” YOWO

Minimum size: 12px cap height on screen, 4mm in print. The mark is always ink on paper or paper on ink. Never anything else:

✕YOWONo shadows
✕YOWONo gradients
✕YOWONo rotation
✕YOWONo outlines
✕YOWONo distortion
✕YOWONo custom tracking
03

Typography

Two voices only. The grotesk speaks; the mono annotates. The grotesk is loud in size but quiet in weight — never bolder than 500. The mono never exceeds 12px and always tracks wide, like marginalia or specimen labels in an archive.

Colour

The static world is monochrome — paper and ink, with two greys for annotation. Colour exists only in motion: the flora cursor and its petals drift through a muted spectrum and are gone. If something coloured stands still, it is wrong.

07

Motion

Motion is the only place where YOWO is allowed to be strange. Every movement borrows from nature — things grow, breathe, drift, flow and fall. Nothing slides, bounces, pops or spins. Four principles:

M1Nothing moves without meaning.No decoration
M2The ecosystem breathes — slowly.Ambient ≥ 3s
M3Motion is organic: grow, drift, flow, fall.Never mechanical
M4Stillness is respected.reduced-motion honoured

Three tiers of motion, three tempos. Live tokens:

Micro / response
0.3–0.4s · cubic-bezier(.6,0,.2,1)
Transition / reveal
0.9–1.6s · cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1)
Ambient / breath
3–90s · ease-in-out · infinite

The signature behaviours, alive below. These are the brand; reuse them before inventing new ones.

Breathe — nodes, 6s
Flow — connections, 50s
Stories
Drift — desk objects, 11–15s
↓
Sap — workflow arrows, 3.4s
Grow — rules, 1.6s once
move your cursor
& click anywhere
Flora — cursor, petals fall on click
Flora cursor Six petals, each pulsing on its own cycle so the flower never repeats a shape. Hue drifts one full circle in ~45s at 42% saturation, ≤ 55% alpha. Blooms +45% over links. Traces fade in 2.8s. The only emblem
Petal fall One petal per click. Falls at 26–48px/s with sinusoidal sway, gentle rotation, gone in 9–12s. A click is a seed: attention becomes a falling, settling thing. Click ritual
Budget Maximum two ambient behaviours per viewport. Ambient opacity change ≤ 60%, travel ≤ 6px. If a visitor notices the motion before the content, reduce it. Restraint rule

Hard prohibitions:

  • No bounce, elastic or spring easings — nothing in nature bounces like a UI library.
  • No parallax heroes, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay carousels.
  • No motion faster than 250ms except the flora cursor itself.
  • No looping attention-seekers: badges, pulsing CTAs, shimmer loaders.
  • No coloured motion outside the flora spectrum.
  • Nothing moves when prefers-reduced-motion is set. Ever.
